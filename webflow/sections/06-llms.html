What LLMs can and can’t do

LLMs are very good at recognizing and reproducing patterns, but they don’t understand things the way humans do.

That distinction explains what these systems are good at and where they go wrong. Treat an LLM’s output as a first draft from a fast, well-read assistant who is sometimes wrong: a useful starting point that a person needs to review.

Strengths

  • Recognizing patterns across large datasets quickly

    Having learned from enormous amounts of text, an LLM can spot themes, repetitions and connections across far more material than a person could read in the same time.

  • Drafting, summarizing and reformatting language

    It can turn rough notes into a first draft, condense a long report into key points, or rewrite content for a different audience or format, such as an email, a list or a table.

  • Translating between languages

    Because it has learned from text in many languages, it can translate quickly and often keep the tone as well as the meaning. Results are weaker for languages with less training data.

  • Handling repetitive analytical work at scale

    It can sort feedback, tag documents or pull details out of forms across thousands of items in minutes. A person still needs to check the results.

Weaknesses

  • It can be confidently wrong

    LLMs sometimes “hallucinate”: they produce false information that reads as entirely plausible. They have no reliable way to signal a gap in their knowledge.10

  • It doesn’t understand or reason the way people do

    Generative AI works by recognizing patterns in existing data. What it produces is built from things people have already made, and it has no lived experience to check its ideas against.12

  • It doesn’t know what’s happening in real time

    Unless it’s connected to live information, a model only knows what was in its training data. The world keeps changing, so an answer based on outdated data can be wrong or irrelevant.10

  • It’s sensitive to how it’s asked

    The same request phrased differently can produce a different answer.13
