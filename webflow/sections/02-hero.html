Artificial intelligence, a “brief” introduction

Artificial intelligence is shaping decisions in policy, business, education and media, and changing the conditions we all live and work under.

Like other advanced technologies, it can both improve people’s lives and cause harm.

At Sandbox Labs, we are committed to helping citizens understand AI. Understanding is the first step towards critical, thoughtful engagement with these tools, and towards support for human-centred models of AI development that serve the public interest.

This page is that first step: a plain-language guide to what AI is, how it works and the risks worth knowing about.
